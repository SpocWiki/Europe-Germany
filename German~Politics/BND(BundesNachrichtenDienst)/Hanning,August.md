> **August Hanning** (born 16 February 1946) 
> is a former high-ranking German security official (civil servant, Beamter) 
> and a businessman in the private security consulting sector. 
> 
> From 1998 to 2005, he served as President of Germany's foreign intelligence agency, 
> the Federal Intelligence Service (BND). 
> 
> He was state secretary at the Federal Ministry of the Interior 
> from 2005 to 2009 until his Retirement.
> 
> On 6 October 2026, Hanning was arrested by BKA 
> on the orders of the Federal Public Prosecutor General on suspicion of 
> - treasonous espionage, 
> - the betrayal of state secrets, 
> - attempted high treason, and 
> - acting as an intelligence agent for a foreign power.
>
